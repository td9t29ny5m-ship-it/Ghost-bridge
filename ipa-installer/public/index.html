Install fails or says "Unable to install"?

iOS only installs IPAs that are signed for your device: an ad-hoc or development build that includes your device's UDID, or an enterprise build. The tags under each app show what was detected.

"App Store encrypted" IPAs (copied from a purchase) can't be installed this way.

Unsigned IPA? Re-sign it with your own Apple ID using a sideloading tool such as AltStore or Sideloadly, or with your Apple Developer account, then upload the signed copy here.

After installing a development or ad-hoc app, you may need to trust the developer under Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, and turn on Developer Mode (Settings → Privacy & Security).

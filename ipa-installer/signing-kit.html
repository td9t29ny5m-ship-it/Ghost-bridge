iPhone Signing Kit

Make the certificate files that let your iPhone run your own IPAs. Everything happens in this page on your phone. Your private key never leaves the device.

request→ Apple→ .cer→ .p12 + profile→ Feather
1

Make a certificate request

to do

Apple needs a request file to issue your certificate. This creates one, plus your private key.

You get SigningRequest.zip. Open it in the Files app to unzip it. Keep private-key.pem safe: it is your backup if this page forgets the key.

2

Get your certificate and profile from Apple

on apple.com

This needs a paid Apple Developer account ($99/year). Sign in at developer.apple.com. If a page looks cramped, tap aA → Request Desktop Website.

  1. Certificate. Certificates → + → Apple Distribution → upload CertificateSigningRequest.certSigningRequest → Download. You get a .cer file.
  2. Your iPhone. Devices → + → paste your iPhone's UDID. To find the UDID with only a phone, open a UDID finder site such as udid.tech in Safari and install its profile, then copy the number it shows.
  3. App ID. Identifiers → + → App IDs → App → Wildcard, Bundle ID *. One wildcard covers any app.
  4. Profile. Profiles → + → Ad Hoc → pick the wildcard App ID, your certificate and your iPhone → Download. You get a .mobileprovision file.

4

Install Feather and your IPAs

on your iPhone
  1. Get Feather on the phone once. In your GitHub repo, go to Actions → Sign IPA → Run workflow. The link box is already filled with Feather. When it finishes, open the GitHub Pages link from the run in Safari and tap Install.
  2. Trust it. Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode on (iPhone restarts), if iOS asks for it.
  3. Add your certificate to Feather. Certificates → + → pick Certificate.p12 and the .mobileprovision from Signing-Files.zip, enter your password.
  4. Install any IPA. In Files, long-press an IPA → Share → Feather (or import it in Feather) → Sign → Install. Big files are fine: they never leave your phone.

Some IPAs still won't open: copies of App Store apps stay locked to the account that bought them, and re-signing doesn't change that. Your certificate and profile last one year.

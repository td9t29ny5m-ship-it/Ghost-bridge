This animal has three hearts, blue blood, and nine brains.
It's the octopus. And it gets weirder.
Number one. Two hearts pump blood to the gills. The third pumps it everywhere else. But when it swims, that heart stops. So it would rather crawl.
Number two. Its blood is blue, because it uses copper, not iron.
Number three. One main brain, plus a mini brain in every arm. Its arms can even taste, just by touching.
And the craziest part? It changes color in less than a second. But it's probably colorblind. Scientists think its skin might sense light.

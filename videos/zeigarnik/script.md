# Ep. 1 — Don't watch this to the end (the Zeigarnik effect)

~50 s · Kokoro am_michael ×1.1 · accent #ec5626 · loops into the opening line

## Structure
- HOOK (0–5 s): "Don't watch this to the end. Seriously. If you quit halfway, your brain might not let it go."
- STORY (5–16 s): Berlin, 1920s: a waiter who remembers every unpaid order, then forgets it once the bill is paid.
- THE EFFECT (16–26 s): Bluma Zeigarnik's test; cliffhangers, unread messages.
- OPEN LOOP → TWIST (27–40 s): the 2025 meta-analysis found the memory advantage doesn't hold up; the urge to resume (Ovsiankina effect) does.
- ACTION (40–46 s): start the thing you're avoiding, two minutes, then stop.
- LOOP (47–50 s): "Kind of like how you just watched this to the end."

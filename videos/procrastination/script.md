# You don't procrastinate because you're lazy

Length target: ~45 s · Voice: Kokoro am_michael · Accent: #ec5626

## Script
- HOOK: You don't procrastinate because you're lazy. You do it to feel better, right now.
- BEAT 1 (mood, not time): Sirois & Pychyl call it a mood problem, not a time problem. The task feels bad, so you dodge the feeling. Instant relief.
- BEAT 2 (dread piles up): But the dread doesn't leave. It piles up.
- BEAT 3 (future self = stranger): Stanford brain scan — future self looks like a stranger. You hand the job to someone you don't know.
- BEAT 4 (the fix): Students who forgave themselves for putting off one exam procrastinated less on the next.
- CLOSE: Forgive yourself. Make the first step tiny. Just start. (stamp: JUST START)

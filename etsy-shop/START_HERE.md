# Your Etsy Printables Shop: Start Here

Everything is ready: **11 listings** (10 planners and 1 bundle). Each one comes with print-ready PDFs (US Letter and A4), 4 listing photos, and a title, tags, price and description you can copy and paste.

Your part takes about 30–45 minutes the first time. After that, Etsy delivers the files automatically every time something sells.

---

## 1. Download the files (2 min)

Download this ZIP on a computer and unzip it:
https://github.com/td9t29ny5m-ship-it/Ghost-bridge/archive/refs/heads/claude/nifty-brahmagupta-4yrt77.zip

Everything you need is in the `etsy-shop` folder:
- `LISTINGS.md` has the title, tags, price and description for every listing.
- `products/<name>/files/` holds the files buyers download.
- `products/<name>/images/` holds the listing photos, in order (1, 2, 3, 4).

## 2. Open your Etsy shop (15–20 min, one time)

1. Go to **etsy.com/sell** and click **Get started**.
2. **Shop name:** pick something simple and calm, like *PaperNest Prints*, *Tidy Day Printables* or *Simply Planned Co*. Check it isn't already taken.
3. Etsy will ask you to create your first listing during setup. Use **Christmas Planner** (see step 3).
4. Add your bank account and verify your ID. Etsy requires this to pay you.
5. You may have to pay a one-time **setup fee (about $15–29, depends on country)**. Listings cost **$0.20 each**, and Etsy takes about **9.5% plus a small fixed fee** per sale.

## 3. Create each listing (2–3 min each)

For each product, open `LISTINGS.md` and copy from it:

| Etsy field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Photos | Upload `1-cover.jpg`, `2-…`, `3-…`, `4-…` from the product's `images` folder, **in order** |
| Title | Copy from LISTINGS.md |
| About this listing | **Who made it:** I did · **What is it:** A finished product · **When was it made:** 2020–2026 |
| Category | Type "planner" or "calendar" and choose **Paper & Party Supplies → Paper → Calendars & Planners** |
| Type | **Digital** |
| Description | Copy from LISTINGS.md |
| Tags | Copy all 13 from LISTINGS.md |
| Price | Copy from LISTINGS.md |
| Digital files | Upload **every** file in the product's `files` folder |
| Renewal | **Automatic** |

**Do these first, in this order.** It's mid-November, and holiday and new-year products sell now:
1. **Christmas Planner**: sells until about 20 December
2. **2027 Calendar**: sells November–February
3. **Everything Bundle**: highest price, best value per sale
4. Budget Planner and Habit Tracker: new-year resolution season
5. Everything else

## 4. Shop settings worth 5 minutes

- **Shop policies:** choose "No returns or exchanges" (standard for digital downloads).
- **About section / shop announcement:** *"Minimal, printer-friendly planners. Instant download in US Letter and A4."*
- Check Etsy's current **Creativity Standards** (Etsy's seller handbook) and include any disclosure they ask for. These designs were made with software tools.

## 5. Optional, but it speeds up the first sales

- **Etsy Ads:** start at **$1–2/day** for the Christmas Planner and Calendar only. New shops get little search visibility, and ads buy your first sales and reviews. Turn them off if they don't pay for themselves within about 2 weeks.
- **Pinterest:** make a free account and pin each `1-cover.jpg` linking to its listing. Pinterest drives a lot of printable sales and needs no further effort.

## What to realistically expect

- Your first sale usually comes within **a few days to a few weeks**, depending on competition and ads.
- These are $3–15 products. Income comes from **volume across many listings**, not one big sale. More listings mean more chances to show up in search.
- When something sells, tell Claude which one, and it can make more products in that niche (e.g. more holiday or budget variations).

## Making more products later

Everything is generated by `etsy-shop/build/build.js` from `products.js`. To add or change products, ask Claude. It can edit `products.js` and run `node build.js` to regenerate everything.

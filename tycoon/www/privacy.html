Summary

Yum Empire does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address or contacts. Your game progress is saved only on your device.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (cash, businesses, settings) is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers. Deleting the app or using "Delete save" in Settings removes it.

Advertising

The app offers optional video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. These are served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location based on IP address, and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads. On iOS you will be asked for permission before the advertising identifier is used for tracking. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to verify purchases; it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts. We never receive your payment details. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you publish it for a younger audience, configure AdMob for child-directed treatment and update this section.
